# Haberdashery Sales Analysis 2023-2026

Exploratory Data Analysis of daily sales from a family-owned business located in the oil industry zone of Veracruz, Mexico.

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 3 years of real daily sales data from a small subsidiary haberdashery business. The goal is to identify sales patterns, seasonality, and actionable insights for business decision-making.

## Key Findings
- October, November and December are consistently the strongest months
- April and May are the weakest months each year
- Monday and Tuesday generate the most revenue
- Open Sundays generate 30% less revenue than other days ($743 vs $1,055)
- Extreme sales peaks concentrate in end-of-year season, consistent with the oil industry payday cycle in the region

## Business Context
- Family business located in an oil industry zone (PEMEX, Braskem IDESA)
- Workers in the area receive biweekly payments, influencing consumer behavior
- A second branch was opened in August-September 2025

## Tech Stack

Real daily sales data from 2023 to 2026. Business name anonymized for privacy.
